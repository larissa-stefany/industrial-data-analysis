# Business Questions

This project is designed around questions that a manufacturing analyst could receive from operations and quality teams.

## Productivity

1. Which machines generate the highest production volume?
2. Which shift has the highest average OEE?
3. Which machines lose the most productive time to downtime?
4. Is production performance improving or declining month over month?

## Quality

1. Which machines have the highest defect rate?
2. What share of all defective units is concentrated in the most critical machines?
3. Are there shifts with systematically worse quality results?
4. Which machine/shift combinations should be prioritized for root-cause analysis?

## Operational performance

1. How do availability, performance and quality contribute to OEE losses?
2. Which machines combine high downtime and low OEE?
3. Where would an improvement initiative have the largest potential impact?

## Decision framework

The analyses prioritize opportunities using three dimensions:

- **Impact:** volume of production or defects affected.
- **Performance gap:** distance from the plant average or target.
- **Concentration:** whether a small group of machines explains a large share of the loss.

This approach turns descriptive analytics into a practical prioritization tool for continuous improvement.
